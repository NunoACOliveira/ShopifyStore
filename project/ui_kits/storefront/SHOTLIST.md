# IKKEN — media to produce

I cannot generate photography. Every `MediaSlot` in the designs is a labelled hole with the brief
written into it, so you can judge the layout now and shoot against this list. Slot label in the design
matches the name here.

**House rules for all of it.** Cool, desaturated, high-key. Daylight or one hard light — never a soft
beauty light. No colour grade, no added grain, no vignette. Near-white ground for anything cut out
(`#e4e9e9` or brighter, evenly lit, so the cut-out reads as floating). Frames must stay light in the
centre so ink type sits on them without a protection gradient.

## 1. Splash / home hero — `HOME HERO` (film)
- Surf breaking over black rock, or ash falling, or smoke off a fire. 12–18s, silent, seamless loop.
- Handheld, slightly overexposed, 21:9 crop safe. Shoot 4K so the crop survives.
- **Centre of frame must stay pale** — `PRESS TO ENTER` and the wordmark sit there in ink.
- Deliver: MP4 (H.264, under 6 MB for Shopify) + a still frame as the poster image.

## 2. On-body editorial, outdoors — `EDITORIAL / OUTDOORS 01–02`
- One piece per frame, worn, full body, figure small in a landscape: rock, salt, low sun, wind.
- 4:5 portrait. No eye contact, no styling props, no poses. Two per colourway is plenty.
- One frame with **no figure at all** — just the ground. It carries the About page.

## 3. On-body editorial, studio — `EDITORIAL / STUDIO 01` and `EDITORIAL / STUDIO — THIS PIECE`
- Same piece, near-white studio ground, shoulders to hip, hands out of frame.
- One 4:5 portrait per colourway **shot identically** — same distance, same light, same crop — so the
  shop grid stays even. This is the single most valuable set for conversion.
- Plus one 21:9 wide crop per colourway for the bottom of the product page.

## 4. Product cut-outs — garments
- You already have the eight colourways front and back. I cut them out programmatically into
  `assets/products/tee-0N-{front,back}.png`; a few edges are soft. **Reshoot or re-mask properly** if
  these go to production.
- For each piece also shoot: collar and shoulder seam, the stamp at 1:1, and a hem/fray detail. The
  four squares under the product image expect four views.

## 5. Ashtray — `ASHTRAY 01`
- On sale at launch, no photography at all yet. Cut-out, three-quarter view, one hard light from the
  left, same near-white ground as the garments. Plus one in use, ash in it, on a real surface.

## 6. More stones — `STONE 04+`
- You have three cut-out stones. They are the brand's metaphor and the gallery grid's placeholder
  object. Five to eight more, all cut out on transparency, gives the shop and the 404 real range.

## 7. Texture plates
- `Background.png` is doing all the work at the moment. One or two more interference / scanner /
  vellum plates in the same near-white key would let pages differ without changing the language.

## Nice to have, not blocking
- Packaging and the woven label (your moodboard has references).
- Atelier: hands in a dye bath, the hand-cut block, pieces drying. `ATELIER 01` on the About page is
  holding space for this. No faces.
